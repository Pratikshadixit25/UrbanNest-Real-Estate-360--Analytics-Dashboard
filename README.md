# UrbanNest – Real Estate 360° Analytics

## Project Overview

UrbanNest Real Estate 360° Analytics is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The project focuses on analyzing real estate business performance, including property inventory, sales, revenue, customer leads, and sales agent performance.

The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboards.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze total property inventory and property status.
- Identify high-revenue-generating locations.
- Understand property sales and revenue trends.
- Evaluate customer lead generation and conversion rates.
- Analyze site visits and booking performance.
- Measure sales agent performance.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of overall real estate business performance, including property inventory, sales, revenue, location-wise performance, and lead conversion.

### Property Analytics
Focuses on property inventory, property status, property types, locations, and price categories.

### Lead Analytics
Analyzes customer leads, lead sources, qualification, site visits, and conversion performance.

### Sales Performance
Evaluates sales trends, revenue generation, and sales agent performance.
